Build a premium, editorial-style D2C beauty brand website for a new luxury lip oil brand.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT a generic ecommerce template.
Do NOT make it look like a basic Shopify store, dropshipping website, cosmetics template, or startup landing page.

The website should feel like a real, established premium beauty brand with strong art direction, sophisticated typography, cinematic visuals, subtle animations, and exceptional attention to detail.

BRAND:
The brand is a modern premium beauty label launching with ONE hero product: a luxury lip oil.

The brand is currently in PRE-LAUNCH, so do not pretend that the product is already available for purchase. The primary goal is to build desire, collect early-access signups, and establish the brand identity.

CORE BRAND FEEL:
- Modern luxury
- Minimal but expressive
- Editorial beauty magazine aesthetic
- Feminine without being overly pink or childish
- Sophisticated
- Sensual but tasteful
- Premium
- Confident
- Clean
- Contemporary
- Instagram/TikTok-native
- High-end fashion/beauty campaign feeling

VISUAL DIRECTION:
Take inspiration from the visual language of premium beauty and fashion brands, but DO NOT copy any existing brand.

Think:
- luxury skincare campaigns
- high-fashion editorials
- premium fragrance websites
- modern cosmetics campaigns
- minimalist architecture
- glossy textures
- glass
- liquid reflections
- soft shadows
- close-up beauty photography
- cinematic product photography

COLOR SYSTEM:
Use a restrained luxury palette.

Primary:
- warm ivory
- soft cream
- deep espresso brown
- muted nude
- subtle champagne

Use one very subtle accent color if needed.

Avoid:
- bright pink
- excessive gradients
- neon
- generic purple
- overly colorful UI
- childish beauty aesthetics

TYPOGRAPHY:
Use a sophisticated serif display font for major headlines and a clean modern sans-serif for body text and UI.

Typography should feel editorial and expensive.

Use large typography and generous whitespace.

LAYOUT:
The website should feel spacious and intentional.

Do not fill every area with content.

Use:
- oversized typography
- asymmetric editorial layouts
- large photography
- full-screen sections
- overlapping elements
- subtle horizontal dividers
- generous margins
- strong visual hierarchy

NAVIGATION:
Create a refined minimal navigation.

Left:
BRAND LOGO

Center or navigation:
Shop
The Story
Journal

Right:
Search
Account
Bag

Since this is pre-launch, the main CTA should be:
GET EARLY ACCESS

Do not make the navigation bulky.

HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE:

1. CINEMATIC HERO

Create an immersive hero section.

Use a high-quality close-up visual of glossy lips / lip oil texture / premium cosmetic packaging.

Large headline:

"YOUR LIPS,
ELEVATED."

Small supporting text:

"A new generation of lip care, made to be seen."

Primary CTA:

"GET EARLY ACCESS"

Secondary CTA:

"DISCOVER THE STORY"

The hero should immediately communicate luxury.

Add subtle entrance animations.

Do NOT use cheesy animations.

---

2. BRAND STATEMENT

Create a large editorial statement section.

Example:

"WE BELIEVE
LIP CARE SHOULD
LOOK AS GOOD
AS IT FEELS."

Use oversized typography with lots of whitespace.

Make this section visually dramatic.

---

3. PRODUCT TEASER

Introduce the hero product without making the website look like a normal product listing.

Show a large premium product render / placeholder image.

Product name:

"[PRODUCT NAME]"

Category:

"LUXURY LIP OIL"

Description:

"A weightless veil of shine, comfort and effortless color."

CTA:

"MEET THE FORMULA"

If the final formulation is not known yet, do not invent ingredient claims.

---

4. TEXTURE / SENSORY SECTION

Create a visually rich section showing the product texture.

Use close-up liquid/gloss imagery.

Use short statements:

"GLASS-LIKE SHINE"

"WEIGHTLESS FEEL"

"EVERYDAY COLOR"

Make each statement appear through subtle scroll-based animation.

Do not make unsupported product claims.

---

5. THE STORY

Create an editorial section explaining why the brand exists.

Headline:

"BEAUTY, WITHOUT THE NOISE."

Supporting copy:

"We're building a beauty brand around one idea:
fewer products, better experiences."

The story should emphasize:
- intentionality
- simplicity
- quality
- modern beauty
- one hero product

Use an editorial image alongside the text.

---

6. INGREDIENTS / FORMULA

Create a sophisticated ingredients section.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT invent ingredients.

Use clearly marked placeholder content until the final manufacturer/formula is confirmed.

Example:

"THE FORMULA"

"Thoughtfully developed for a comfortable, glossy finish."

Include placeholder ingredient cards that can later be replaced with verified information.

Do not make medical, therapeutic, or exaggerated claims.

---

7. SHADE / PRODUCT EXPERIENCE

Design a premium interactive product section.

Show the lip oil in close-up.

If the launch begins with one shade, display:

01 — [SHADE NAME]

Include an interactive hover or swipe effect.

Make this feel like a beauty campaign rather than a product grid.

---

8. SOCIAL PROOF / COMMUNITY

Because this is a new brand and does not yet have customer reviews, DO NOT create fake testimonials.

Instead create:

"JOIN THE FIRST DROP"

"Be among the first to experience it."

Show a counter or visual indicator:

"FIRST 100"

But don't falsely claim that 100 people have already joined.

---

9. EARLY ACCESS SIGNUP

This is one of the most important sections.

Create a beautiful premium signup form.

Headline:

"BE FIRST."

Text:

"Join the first drop and receive early access when we launch."

Fields:

First name
Email
WhatsApp number (optional)

CTA:

"JOIN THE LIST"

After submission show:

"YOU'RE IN."

"Watch your inbox. Something beautiful is coming."

Do not require unnecessary information.

---

10. INSTAGRAM / SOCIAL SECTION

Create an editorial Instagram-style section.

Heading:

"FOLLOW THE JOURNEY"

Show a grid of 6–9 visual tiles.

These should feel like:
- campaign photography
- behind-the-scenes
- textures
- packaging
- beauty closeups
- lifestyle imagery

Link to the actual Instagram account.

Do not create fake social engagement numbers.

---

11. JOURNAL

Create a small editorial journal section.

Example articles:

"Why we're starting with one product"

"What makes a lip oil different?"

"The art of effortless shine"

"Building a beauty brand from scratch"

The journal should help SEO and establish brand personality.

---

12. FINAL CTA

End with a dramatic full-screen section.

Headline:

"READY FOR
THE FIRST DROP?"

CTA:

"GET EARLY ACCESS"

Use a beautiful dark/cream visual transition.

---

Keep it minimal and premium.

Include:

Shop

Journal

Shipping

Instagram

Newsletter signup.

Add:

© [BRAND NAME]

Do not clutter the footer.

INTERACTIONS:

Use subtle premium micro-interactions:

- smooth page transitions
- elegant hover states
- image reveal animations
- text reveal on scroll
- subtle parallax
- smooth image scaling
- button hover animations
- cursor interactions where appropriate
- sticky navigation that changes appearance after scrolling

Animations should be:
FAST
SMOOTH
SUBTLE
PREMIUM

Avoid:
- bouncing elements
- excessive motion
- flashy gradients
- spinning objects
- gimmicky cursor effects
- excessive loading animations

MOBILE EXPERIENCE:

The mobile version is extremely important.

Design mobile intentionally rather than simply shrinking desktop.

Ensure:
- excellent typography
- fast loading
- thumb-friendly buttons
- clean navigation
- beautiful product imagery
- no horizontal overflow
- excellent spacing
- signup form is easy to use

PERFORMANCE:

Despite the premium visuals, the website must be fast.

Optimize:
- images
- lazy loading
- responsive images
- animations
- fonts
- JavaScript

Do not sacrifice performance for visual effects.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:

Build reusable components.

Use clean, maintainable architecture.

Make the following content configurable:
- brand name
- logo
- product name
- product images
- product description
- price
- shade
- ingredients
- social links
- email signup destination

The website should be easy to convert from PRE-LAUNCH mode to FULL ECOMMERCE mode later.

PRE-LAUNCH MODE:
Primary CTA = GET EARLY ACCESS

FULL LAUNCH MODE:
Primary CTA = SHOP NOW

Do not build fake checkout functionality during pre-launch.

SEO:
Include:
- semantic HTML
- proper title/meta descriptions
- Open Graph metadata
- structured headings
- optimized image alt text
- sitemap-ready architecture
- clean URLs
- fast loading
- mobile-first design

ANALYTICS:
Prepare the website for analytics tracking.

Track:
- page views
- CTA clicks
- signup conversions
- Instagram clicks
- scroll depth
- product interactions

IMPORTANT BRAND RULE:

The website should feel like a brand people WANT to belong to, not a website trying to sell them something.

The final result should look like a premium beauty campaign that happens to be an ecommerce website.

Think:
EDITORIAL + BEAUTY + LUXURY + CULTURE + PRODUCT.

NOT:
GENERIC ECOMMERCE + TEMPLATE + DROPSHIPPING.

Make the final design visually striking enough that someone landing on it for the first time immediately thinks:

"This looks like a serious beauty brand."
